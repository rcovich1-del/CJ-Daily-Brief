7 Strategy in Focus — Bear Call Spread

Chosen by regime (DEFENSE) and weekday. A defined-risk credit structure with a bearish-to-neutral bias — no bullish featured strategy while SPY sits below its 50-day.

The idea (delta / % terms): sell a call around the 30-delta — roughly one expected move above spot — and buy a further-OTM call around the 15-delta for protection, same expiry, ~30–45 DTE. On a typical setup that collects a net credit near 25–35% of the strike width. You keep the full credit if the stock finishes below the short call; breakeven is the short strike plus the credit received. It works best when IV Rank is ≥40% (premium is rich) and price is capped beneath a falling 20- and 50-day.

Main risk: the payoff is defined but asymmetric — a sharp rally can push through both strikes for the maximum loss, which is larger than the credit collected (strike width minus credit). This week that tail is live: a dovish Fed surprise Wednesday or a blow-out MSFT/META print could gap a name straight through the spread, and an in-the-money short call can be assigned early around an ex-dividend date. Size small into the event, and treat the credit as the reward for accepting that capped-but-real upside risk — not as free money.
