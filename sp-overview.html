On the week equities fell -1.48% cap-weighted, the 10-year yield rose 1bp, high yield gained on investment grade (+0.06%), the dollar proxy softened -0.75%. Credit appetite firmed and yields rose while equities fell, which is not a flight to quality. Read together these point to a rates-driven rotation — concentrated in Information Technology — rather than a broad withdrawal from risk. The largest cross-asset move by far is crude: the WTI fund +6.4% on the week and the Brent fund +6.2%. That is corroborated inside the index rather than asserted: Energy was the strongest sector at +2.45% equal-weight, so the move shows up in the equity tape and the commodity tape at the same time.
