Its T8 leg is a variance-risk-premium z-score built from option-chain data that the vendor publishes the following morning, so on the newest bar that leg is still pending and the ensemble cannot total. The transition model is running in shadow. Its vote is pinned to zero in the model configuration and contributes nothing to the E90 score, so the state probabilities above are an unwired forward test rather than a live input. A high bull reading beside a more cautious headline tag is therefore not two signals disagreeing — one of them is not voting yet.
